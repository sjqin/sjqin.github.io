This paper focuses on the clean numerical simulation (CNS) of a three-dimensional turbulent Kolmogorov flow. By comparing the CNS benchmark solutions with direct numerical simulation (DNS) results, the study reveals that the spatial-temporal trajectories given by DNS are often polluted by numerical noise rather quickly, which significantly influences the spatial symmetry and energy cascade of the flow field.
