This work raises a fundamental question about the traditional trust placed in Direct Numerical Simulation (DNS). Through rigorous comparisons, it questions whether merely reducing grid spacing and time-step is sufficient to guarantee the reliability of Navier-Stokes simulations in chaotic regimes.
