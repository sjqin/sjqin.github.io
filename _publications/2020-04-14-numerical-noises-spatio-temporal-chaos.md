This study reveals the profound impacts of inherent computer numerical noise (such as truncation and rounding errors) on the simulation of spatio-temporal chaos. By comparing traditional DNS with CNS, the paper demonstrates the necessity of controlling numerical noise for reliable chaotic simulations.
