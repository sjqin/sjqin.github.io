This paper investigates the Lagrangian chaos properties of the Arnold-Beltrami-Childress (ABC) flow. Employing the Clean Numerical Simulation (CNS), the study deeply explores how particle trajectories behave within complex 3D kinematic flows, revealing profound characteristics of fluid mixing and chaotic advection.
