For more details, please see [*Physics of Fluids* (2025), 37(10), 105160](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0291128)" collection: portfolio --- This paper focuses on the Clean Numerical Simulation (CNS) of a three-dimensional (3D) turbulent Kolmogorov flow. By comparing the CNS benchmark solution with Direct Numerical Simulation (DNS) result, the study reveals that the spatial-temporal trajectories given by DNS are often polluted by numerical noise rather quickly, which significantly influences the spatial symmetry and energy cascade of the flow field.
